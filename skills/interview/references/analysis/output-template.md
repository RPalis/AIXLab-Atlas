# Output template

The structure of the formal deliverable. Compare your output against this sample for better
results. Every format (the Markdown report, a CSV evidence log, a slide version) carries the same content; none may drop
confidence tags, interview numbers, or weak-signal flags.

---

## Header block
- **COMPANY / PROJECT:** [name]
- **TEAM / RESEARCHER:** [name]
- **DATE AND TIME:**
- **TARGET USERS:**

## Analysis logical structure
Survey results · Affinity map · Open-ended questions · Close-ended questions · Post questions ·
Evidence · Findings · Metrics · Recommendations.

## Survey results
[question / answers / results / evidence]

## Affinity map
`(n) out of (N) interviews chose (x) and (y)` — one row per pattern, with confidence tag.

## From open-ended questions
Extract pain-point patterns **by severity**, plus any other positive or negative pattern.

## From close-ended + post questions
Extract the metrics and patterns. `x or y interviews chose z, so then …`

## Per theme (affinity map)
```
THEME: [name] — seen in [n] interviews
EVIDENCE: "[verbatim quote]" — interview [#]
SO WHAT: one line on why it matters
IMPACT: […]
RECOMMENDATION: […]
Confidence: Verified / Weak Signal / Not Verified
```

## Final takeaways
The three-to-five things a reader must remember.

---

## Worked example (from the card)
```
DATE AND TIME: 13/07/2020
COMPANY CLIENT: [ACME]
TEAM / RESEARCHER: [ACME design team]
PROJECT: [Check-out implementation]

THEME: Fear of hidden fees — seen in 4 interviews
EVIDENCE: "I always expect a surprise at checkout" — #3
SO WHAT: price transparency is a trust lever for us.
IMPACT: […]
RECOMMENDATION: Explore progressive explicit-disclosure patterns.
Confidence: Verified
```

---

## The six final outputs (always produced)
1. **Executive Summary** — pain points, needs and workarounds grouped into named themes, each with
   verbatim quotes and interview numbers.
2. **Themed Insights** — the full set of named themes with their evidence.
3. **Pain Point List** — a consolidated list of what is not working, ready to prioritise.
4. **Surprises + Contradictions** — signals that challenge assumptions or conflict across
   interviews.
5. **Weak-Signal Flags** — themes with fewer than two supporting quotes, marked so they are not
   over-read.
6. **Evidence Log** — every quote tied to its interview number, so any synthesis can cite it.

## Links
Add links to raw files / transcripts and/or videos for every cited source.
