# Discovery Interview Questions: [Project Name]

Open-ended, exploratory. Goal: understand the user's world before proposing anything.

## Warm-up (context, not analysis)
- Walk me through your role: what does a typical day/week look like?
- What tools are you using today to get this done?

## Current workflow
- Walk me through the last time you [did the core task this product addresses], start to finish, in as much detail as you can remember.
- What's the most frustrating part of that process? Where do you lose the most time?
- Where do you have to switch between different tools/systems to get one thing done?

## Goals & success
- What does "a good day" look like for you in this part of your work?
- How do you know when you've done this task well? What are you being measured against?

## Pain points & workarounds
- What have you built yourself (a spreadsheet, a personal system) to work around something the existing tools don't do?
- What's something you've just given up trying to do because it's too hard?
