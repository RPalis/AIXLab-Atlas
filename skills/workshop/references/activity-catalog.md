# Workshop Activity Catalog

Real activities, real time ranges, real instructions, vendored so this skill doesn't depend on any specific external FigJam file existing. Each is tagged with the phase(s) it fits best (Discover/Decide/Design/Deliver), but tags are a *default suggestion*, not a hard gate. A deep-dive workshop on one concept can pull any activity regardless of phase.

## Intros
**Time**: 10-20 min · **Phase**: Discover (kickoff/icebreaker)
Each participant adds their name to a character, then places their note on several scales to indicate where they see themselves (e.g. optimist/skeptic, detail/big-picture). Discuss the spread as a group. Use to surface team dynamics before diving into content.

## Hopes & Fears
**Time**: 15-30 min · **Phase**: Discover
Two zones: Hopes and Fears. Each participant writes one hope or fear per sticky note about the project and places it in the matching zone. Diverge individually, then playback and synthesize themes as a group. Good early-session temperature check.

## Stakeholder Map
**Time**: 30-60 min · **Phase**: Discover
Diverge on identifying stakeholders (teams, roles, leads, executives, partners, customers, end users), one per sticky note, each paired with a second note capturing their perspective/expectations in quote form. Cluster into groups, label them, and draw/label relationship lines (influence, process, dependency) between groups.

## Empathy Map
**Time**: 30-60 min · **Phase**: Discover
Four quadrants (Says, Does, Thinks, Feels) around a named user persona at the center. Diverge with one observation per sticky note in the matching quadrant. Extend with the persona's Responsibilities, Goals, and Challenges alongside the map. Annotate unresolved assumptions/questions for later validation.

## Scenario Map
**Time**: 60-90 min · **Phase**: Discover / Decide
Four rows (Phases, Doing, Thinking, Feeling) across as many columns as the scenario needs. Fill in phases first, then add Doing/Thinking/Feeling notes per phase in parallel. Iterate at increasing resolution until comfortable with the detail level. Label unknowns for later inquiry.

## Needs Statements
**Time**: 30-60 min · **Phase**: Decide
Fill in the template: *"The [user] needs a way to [action] so that [benefit]."* Focus on the user's pain points, not features. Ask "what does my user really seek?" More than one needs statement can come from a single pain point. Cluster similar statements and discuss.

## Assumptions & Questions
**Time**: 30-90 min · **Phase**: Decide
A 2×2 grid: Certain/Uncertain × Low-risk/High-risk. Diverge with one assumption or question per sticky note, then individually plot each on the grid. Discuss and reposition as a group. Focus discussion on the upper-right quadrant (high-risk, uncertain). These need validation most urgently.

## Prioritization Grid
**Time**: 30-90 min · **Phase**: Decide
A 2×2 grid: Importance to the user × Feasibility for the team. Plot items as a group, then discuss and reposition relative to each other. Don't over-discuss items that land in the clearly "unwise" zone unless the group believes they're mis-categorized.

## Desired Outcomes
**Time**: 30-60 min · **Phase**: Decide / Design
Three columns: Must-have features, Nice-to-have features, Future iterations. Start ideas in the Nice-to-have column phrased as "Our solution will..." / "Our user will be able to...", then scope down to Must-have by asking what's most essential. Discuss and reposition as a group.

## Big Idea Vignettes
**Time**: 30-60 min · **Phase**: Design
Pairs of sticky notes: one with a brief written idea/headline, one with a quick visual sketch (a single storyboard frame). Diverge on many vignette pairs, share quickly, cluster similar ideas, and converge on a set to take deeper via Scenario Map or Storyboarding.

## Storyboarding
**Time**: 20-60 min · **Phase**: Design
Six sticky-note "frames" per person, each with a quick sketch and caption, forming a beginning/middle/end story. Share stories, get feedback, then converge by weaving the best parts of each teammate's story into one refined "master" story.

## Experience-Based Roadmap
**Time**: 60-90 min · **Phase**: Deliver
Three columns: Near-Term, Mid-Term, Long-Term. Start ideas in Long-Term phrased as "Our user can/will be able to...", then scope down to Mid/Near-Term by asking what's most essential right now vs. later.

## Feedback Grid
**Time**: 30-60 min · **Phase**: Deliver
Four quadrants: Things that worked, Things to change, New ideas to try, Questions we still have. Add specific, constructive notes to the relevant quadrant. Cluster similar notes and discuss patterns/themes. Good end-of-workshop or post-milestone retro.

## ROAM Risks
**Time**: 30-60 min · **Phase**: Decide / Deliver
As risks surface, add them to a holding area, then as a group place each into one of four categories: **Resolved** (not a real threat), **Owned** (a team member owns tracking/handling it), **Accepted** (can't be resolved, must be lived with), **Mitigated** (needs an active plan to eliminate). Add owners/next-steps per risk as placed.
